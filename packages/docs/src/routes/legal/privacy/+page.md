**Last updated:** October 21, 2026

## 1. Scope of This Policy

This Privacy Policy applies to FUTO Cloud ("we", "our", or "us") and covers our collection, use, and disclosure of your information when you create a FUTO Account at futo.cloud and use FUTO Cloud services with it, including the Immich Backup service. This Policy does not cover any third-party websites, services, or applications that can be accessed through our app. Data on your Immich server stays on your server, and we only receive what the Backup Service transmits to us.

## 2. Information We Collect

**Encrypted Backup Data:** Your backup content is end-to-end encrypted on your device before it is uploaded. We store only the encrypted data and do not have your keys, so we cannot read it.

**Account:** When you create or sign in to a FUTO Account at futo.cloud we collect:

- Name
- Email address
- IP address
- Timing metrics

**Purchases:** When you purchase a subscription we collect:

- Country of origin
- Postal code
- Billing address
- Payment card details

Note: Payment card details are collected and stored by our payment processor. We receive only the card brand, last four digits, and expiration date.

**Backup Service:** When your devices connect to futo.cloud we may collect the following information about the request:

- IP address
- User-Agent and client version
- Storage used, and the number and size of encrypted objects
- Timing metrics
- Client side logs of the backup process
- Connection name (your configured external host or machine hostname) and connection type
- Backup names you provide

Connection and backup names are not part of your encrypted backup content. We store them unencrypted so we can show them in your account.

**Device and Connection Information:** To optimize FUTO Cloud for the devices and networks our users are on, we may also collect:

- Operating system
- Hardware specifications
- Network information (e.g., ASN and measured upload/download speeds)

## 3. Use of Your Information

**Providing FUTO Cloud:** Account information is used to create and secure your account, store and return your backups, bill your subscription, and respond to support requests.

**Purchasing:** The country of origin and postal code are collected solely for determining the applicable tax rates on your purchase. Card details are used by our payment processor to process your payment.

**Improving FUTO Cloud:** Request metadata, device, and connection information help us understand our user base and ensure that we optimize our clients and infrastructure for those devices, networks, and locations.

## 4. Sharing of Your Information

**Tax Authorities:** The purchase information may be shared with tax authorities as required by law.

**Payment Providers:** The purchase information may be shared with payment providers where required.

**Infrastructure Providers:** Our hosting provider stores data on our behalf and may only process it to provide that service to us.

**Legal Requests:** We disclose information only where legally required, such as a lawful request by public authorities. Because backup content is end-to-end encrypted, we cannot produce it.

We do not sell your information, and we do not use it for advertising.

## 5. Data Retention and Deletion

We keep your information while your account is active. Once your subscription ends (a cancellation takes effect at the end of the current billing period) or you delete your account, your backup data will no longer be available for export and is deleted from our systems within 90 days. Billing records are kept as long as tax law requires.

## 6. Your Rights

You can view and update your account information in your account settings. To access, export, correct, or delete your information, contact us at [support@futo.tech](mailto:support@futo.tech) we honor the rights granted by the privacy laws that apply to you (such as the GDPR and CCPA).

## 7. Security

We're committed to safeguarding your information. While no system is 100% secure, we take various measures to protect information about you from unauthorized access, use, alteration, or destruction. Your keys are yours alone: we cannot reset or recover them, so keep them safe.

## 8. Changes to This Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. If we make any changes, we will notify you by revising the "Last updated" date at the top of this policy. If the changes are material, we will also notify you by email to your account address.

## 9. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at [support@futo.tech](mailto:support@futo.tech)
